1. Introduction
Welcome to AstroBeat: Fluids & Music ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles permissions, device data, and user information.
2. Information Collection and Storage
AstroBeat: Fluids & Music is designed to operate locally on your Android device.
• No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contact list.
• No Remote Servers: All fluid physics simulations, custom presets, and equalizer settings are stored and processed 100% locally on your device.
3. Application Permissions and Usage
Our application requests specific Android device permissions solely to provide interactive features. Here is how each permission is used:
A. Microphone Permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)
• Purpose: Used strictly for real-time frequency analysis when you enable the "Room Mic Reactive" mode in the Music Visualizer or Fluid Lab.
• Data Safety: Audio input is analyzed in device temporary memory (RAM) in real time to generate visual waveforms and fluid motion. No audio recordings are ever recorded, saved, stored, or transmitted off your device.
B. Storage & Audio Media Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO)
• Purpose: Used to let you browse, select, and play local audio files (MP3, WAV, OGG) stored on your phone inside the built-in Music Visualizer player.
• Data Safety: Media files remain on your device and are never uploaded or shared.
C. Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE)
• Purpose: Provides subtle tactile haptic vibration feedback when you touch and sculpt fluid swirls on the screen.
D. Live Wallpaper Service (android.software.live_wallpaper)
• Purpose: Allows you to apply interactive fluid simulations and 3D animated planets as your Android Home & Lock Screen Live Wallpaper.

5. Third-Party Services
